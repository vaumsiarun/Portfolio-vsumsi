CAPSTONE SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE

Hospital Bed Allocation and Availability Monitoring System

A concept overview of the project modules and data lifecycle.

Problem Statement

During busy emergency periods, healthcare facilities can struggle with delayed bed turnover, manual status checks, and limited visibility into cleaned versus occupied beds.

Key Architectural Components

  1. Bed Inventory Engine: Tracks ICU, CCU, Emergency, and General ward capacity.
  2. Patient Admission Dispatcher: Matches patient needs to suitable available beds.
  3. Discharge & Sanitization: Tracks beds through occupied, cleaning, and ready states.
  4. Emergency Priority: Flags critical cases requiring urgent allocation.
